The story

Small salon. Big dreams.

My passion, your confidence.

Hi, I’m Aleigha — a 19-year-old fully qualified hairdresser with three years of hands-on work and a diploma earned at the top of my class. Doing hair isn’t a side thing. It’s the job.

Right now I come to you. Home visits across Saintfield, Downpatrick and nearby County Down, with professional products and the same care you’d expect in a salon chair. Every penny from those appointments goes toward the at-home salon I’m building in Saintfield — a small room where people leave feeling their best.

Don’t let the age fool you. The standard is the work: the blend, the finish, and how you feel when you look in the mirror.
